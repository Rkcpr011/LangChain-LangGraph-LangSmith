🏏 Cricket Basics

Cricket is a bat-and-ball game played between two teams of 11 players each.
It originated in England and is popular in countries like India, Australia, England, Pakistan, and South Africa.

📋 Formats of the Game:
1. Test Cricket - 5 Days
2. One Day International (ODI) - 50 overs
3. Twenty20 (T20) - 20 overs

🏆 Popular Tournaments:
- ICC Cricket World Cup
- ICC T20 World Cup
- Indian Premier League (IPL)
- The Ashes

🎯 Basic Rules:
- One team bats and tries to score runs
- The other team bowls and fields to restrict runs and dismiss batters
- After a set number of overs or dismissals, teams switch roles
